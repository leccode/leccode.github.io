About Me

I am Constantine Lekkos, a Software Engineer and Biomedical Engineer driven by a deep curiosity for how technology can solve complex problems in science and industry.

Currently, I am pursuing an MPhil in Computer Science and Informatics, where my research focuses on Physics-Informed Neural Networks, Quantum Computing, and Hardware approaches in Bioinformatics. I also hold an MEng in Biomedical Engineering.

Professionally, I have spent over 4 years building reliable backend and full-stack production systems across fintech, healthcare, and logistics. I specialize in designing clean RESTful APIs and scalable web applications using .NET/C#, Blazor, Rust (Switching), and modern JavaScript frameworks. I am currently working in a clinic.

When I'm not writing code, I am usually reading about the intersection of quantum mechanics and deep learning, or working on my next project.
